Your background records the work, hardship, or calling that shaped you before you became an adventurer. Each entry pairs an attribute boost with a skill and a starting item.

## Background gains

- **Attribute boost:** The attribute improved by the background. The supplied rules identify the attribute but do not state the boost amount.
- **Skill gained:** Follow the skill link to its uses and advantages.
- **Starting item / effect:** Equipment or a companion gained from your former life, including any stated use or effect.
