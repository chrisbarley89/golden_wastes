Skills describe the areas where your character has useful training, from climbing a wall to tending wounds or finding a hidden door. Your [class](/categories/class/) and [background](/character/backgrounds/) help establish that training.

## Reading the skill table

- **Attribute:** The attribute associated with that skill entry.
- **Skilled modifier:** The modifier recorded for a trained character; every listed skill has +1.
- **Uses and advantages:** What the skill covers, including particular benefits and restrictions. These descriptions do not imply automatic advantage on every check.

Animal Lore, Athletics, Gather Info, and Watercraft each have two attribute variants. Both entries are listed so their attributes remain explicit.
