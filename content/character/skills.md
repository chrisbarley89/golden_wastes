<div class="rules-table rules-table--directory" role="region" aria-label="Skills" tabindex="0"><table aria-label="Skills"><thead><tr><th scope="col">Skill</th><th scope="col">Uses</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-acrobatics">Acrobatics</h3></th><td><p>Climbing, jumping, tumbling, parkour, escapology, and tests of agility. May assist with standing up from <strong>Prone</strong></p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-animal-lore">Animal Lore</h3></th><td><p>Animal behaviour, animal handling, hunting, mounted combat, driving carts &amp; carriages, animal training, veterinary lore, bestial monster lore.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-apothecary">Apothecary</h3></th><td><p>First aid (including using Healer’s Kits), injury recovery, poisons, biology, treating disease.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-arcane-lore">Arcane Lore</h3></th><td><p>Sensing magic, recalling magical lore, fantastic monster lore, alchemy. Might assist in disarming some kinds of magical traps (<em>GM’s call</em>).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-athletics">Athletics</h3></th><td><p>Running, climbing, swimming, jumping, tests of physical power including grappling, breaking down a door, knocking an enemy <strong>prone</strong>, pushing a foe backwards, and so on.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-deception">Deception</h3></th><td><p>Telling convincing lies, impersonations, making and using disguises and forgeries. <strong>NPCs</strong> might oppose using <strong>Int</strong> (<em>GM’s call</em>).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-detection">Detection</h3></th><td><p>General state of alertness as well as purposeful searching. Detecting traps, ambushes, clues, secret doors, and so on.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-divine-lore">Divine Lore</h3></th><td><p>Specialized, obscure, or forbidden religious lore, rites, and creatures (holy and unholy).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-gather-info">Gather Info</h3></th><td><p>Rumour hunting, rumour mongering, canvassing for witnesses or a missing person or item, extended researching (eg: monster weaknesses or other specifics, the location of a lost ruin, etc).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-general-lore">General Lore</h3></th><td><p>The general catch all lore skill, recalling a broad range of historical, scientific, and political information (but not Arcane, Animal, Divine or <strong>Wilderness Lore</strong>).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-leadership">Leadership</h3></th><td><p>Inspiration, bolstering morale, intimidation, teaching, having orders obeyed. The <strong>Leadership</strong> skill assists in recruiting hirelings.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-persuasion">Persuasion</h3></th><td><p>Debating, convincing, negotiation, diplomacy, etiquette</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-sleight-of-hand">Sleight of Hand</h3></th><td><p>Picking pockets, palming, hiding small objects on your person, or planting objects on others.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-stealth">Stealth</h3></th><td><p>Hiding and moving silently. Often opposed by <strong>Perc</strong> (<strong>Detection</strong>).</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-traps-locks">Traps &amp; Locks</h3></th><td><p>Picking non-magical locks, setting and disarming non-magical traps. Noticing traps is covered by <strong>Perc</strong> (<strong>Detection</strong>). Some traps may require the character to be skilled in Traps &amp; Locks to attempt a disarm.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-watercraft">Watercraft</h3></th><td><p>Navigating and operating ships (including airships if applicable), rafts, canoes, etc. Also includes fishing and swimming.</p></td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row"><h3 id="skill-wilderness-lore">Wilderness Lore</h3></th><td><p>Wilderness survival, herbalism, foraging, hunting game, tracking. Might include some relevant non-magical monster lore (<em>GM’s call</em>).</p></td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
