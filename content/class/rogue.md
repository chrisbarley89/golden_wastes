<p class="rules-lead">Speedy and silent assassin</p>

<div class="rules-prose"><p>You are a <strong>rogue</strong>, a lurker in the shadows, gifted in acrobatics, stealth, and sleight of hand. <em>You might be a brave scout, a sly thief, a deadly assassin, or a haughty pirate.</em> In any guise, agility and cunning are the rogue’s hallmarks.</p>
<p>Lightly armoured, you rely on speed and guile to keep you from harm, employing a range of tricks and deceits to maintain the upper hand. When you strike, your sword comes without warning, dropping opponents before they realise the true threat on the battlefield.</p></div></div>

<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Backstab</h3>

<p>You are a master of ambush and assassination. From <strong>1st level</strong>, once per turn, when making a melee attack against a target that is <strong>surprised</strong> by you, gain a <strong>+4 attack bonus</strong> and inflict an additional <code>1d8</code> damage. At <strong>5th level</strong> the damage increases to <code>3d6</code>.</p>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Finisher</h3>

<p>From <strong>1st level</strong> you are adept at picking off injured foes. When you hit a <strong>Wounded</strong> target (p.83), you may add your backstab damage (once per target, and melee attacks only).</p>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-3">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-3"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Tricks</h3>

<p>You come prepared with surprises up your sleeve, ready to exploit any <strong>advantage</strong> or opportunity. At <strong>1st level</strong>, you know a number of tricks equal to your <strong>Dex mod</strong>; roll <code>1d12</code> on the below list to determine which. With each new level, learn one additional trick of your choosing.</p>
<p>You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. Unless noted otherwise, you may expend a use of this ability as part of your normal action, choosing one trick to apply. If a trick requires a <strong>Dex check</strong>, you may use a <strong>Reroll</strong>. You may regain expended uses by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>.</p>

<div class="rules-talents">
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
<h4 id="ability-cat-s-grace-c3d31e8d0b2a">Cat&#x27;s Grace</h4>
<p>Subject to <em>GM discretion</em>, you may turn a failed save or check relating to climbing, acrobatics, or falling, into a success.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
<h4 id="ability-choking-dust-3e8f73418a3f">Choking Dust</h4>
<p>Up to <code>1d4</code> targets within <strong>Melee range</strong> must make a <strong>Luck (Con) save</strong> or be rendered mute for <code>1d4</code> rounds.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
<h4 id="ability-blind-sense-e5876200e3f7">Blind Sense</h4>
<p>By applying the proper techniques, you temporarily heighten your senses, downgrading <strong>Blind</strong> to <strong>Near Blind</strong>, or negating <strong>Near Blind</strong>. The effect lasts <code>1d6 x 10</code> minutes.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
<h4 id="ability-flash-powder-6cc2641f25de">Flash Powder</h4>
<p>Up to <code>1d4</code> targets within <strong>Melee range</strong> must make a <strong>Luck (Dex) save</strong> or be <strong>Blind</strong> until the end of their next turn.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">05</span>
<h4 id="ability-glue-pot-f38d8de179d1">Glue Pot</h4>
<p>On a failed <strong>Luck (Str) save</strong>, one target within <strong>Far range</strong> is <strong>Grabbed</strong> (<strong>Str 14</strong>).</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">06</span>
<h4 id="ability-hidden-blade-c79139d3f7b6">Hidden Blade</h4>
<p>You may reroll a failed melee attack.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">07</span>
<h4 id="ability-quick-reflexes-344343ccec61">Quick Reflexes</h4>
<p>You may reroll your <strong>Initiative check</strong> and use the best result.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">08</span>
<h4 id="ability-rapid-dose-923087beb3c5">Rapid Dose</h4>
<p>You may apply a single dose of poison, or drink a potion (or similar).</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">09</span>
<h4 id="ability-slippery-mind-91049c90d253">Slippery Mind</h4>
<p>When subject to magical forces that control or detect your thoughts, or locate you, you may choose to make a <strong>Will check</strong> to fool, mislead, or negate the effect. This ability lasts <code>2d6</code> minutes.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">10</span>
<h4 id="ability-smoke-bomb-bae3541c8f8e">Smoke Bomb</h4>
<p>You throw a smoke bomb up to <strong>Far range</strong>. Thick smoke fills a Close area, blocking line of sight and rendering those within <strong>Blind</strong>. The smoke disperses in <code>1d4</code> rounds.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">11</span>
<h4 id="ability-unseen-whip-55fe408b8fb9">Unseen Whip</h4>
<p>On a failed <strong>Luck (Dex) save</strong>, one target within <strong>Melee range</strong> is tripped, pulled a short distance (<strong>GM</strong> determines how far), or disarmed.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">12</span>
<h4 id="ability-recon-leader-ddb0a6fb982e">Recon Leader</h4>
<p>If an ally within <strong>Close range</strong> fails a stealth related roll, you may trigger this ability to cause them to roll again and apply the best result.</p>
</section>
