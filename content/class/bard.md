<p class="rules-lead">Charismatic wanderer, artist, and diplomat</p>

<div class="rules-prose"><p>You are a <strong>bard</strong>, a charismatic wanderer; part artist, diplomat, and warrior, as skilled with blades as you are with ballads. <em>You might be a fearsome skald, a celebrated performer, or a master of whispers.</em> In your travels you draw knowledge from all corners, forging alliances and extracting secrets from those who should know better.</p>
<p>A bard’s influence fortifies the company, strengthening bonds and the common will to prevail. With the right words and deeds, the bard elevates the party beyond the sum of its parts.</p></div></div>

<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Silver Tongued</h3>

<p>At <strong>1st level</strong> you have the gift of the gab, and are skilled in the art of soliciting rumours, gossip, and other intel. Gain <strong>advantage</strong> when using the <strong>Gather Information</strong> skill, when performing to entertain or inspire, and when using either the <strong>Persuasion</strong> or <strong>Deception</strong> skill (choose one).</p>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Inspire Greatness</h3>

<p>At <strong>1st level</strong>, your unifying presence builds trust within the party and inspires its companions to moments of greatness. You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. You may use <em>Inspire Greatness</em> at any time <em>(no action)</em> provided an ally is within <strong>Far range</strong>, and able to see or hear you. When you inspire an ally, grant them one of the following effects. Expended uses may be regained by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Apply a bonus equal to your <strong>Cha modifier</strong> on any single roll <em>(excluding Trauma rolls)</em>. You may use this ability after seeing the result of the roll. You may use this ability on your own rolls if you can see or hear an ally within <strong>Far range</strong>. The <strong>GM</strong> may limit the kinds of rolls this ability applies to and/or if it stacks with other modifiers p.64.</li>
<li>If an ally has suffered an adverse magical effect for at least one round, they may attempt a second <strong>Luck save</strong> to end it. If successful, the <strong>PC</strong>’s <strong>Luck score</strong> is reduced by two points instead of one. This ability is not applicable to instant durations or permanent effects, and may not be activated more than once per round or effect.</li>
<li>If an ally is reduced to zero <strong>hp</strong>, that ally gains an immediate melee or ranged attack against their opponent. The ally is reduced to zero <strong>hp</strong> regardless of the outcome of their attack (any hits occur simultaneously, which may lead to both combatants being incapacitated).</li>
</ul>

</section>

</section>

<p>By <strong>2nd level</strong>, your studies have earned you an ear for languages and a broad knowledge of cultures. You may make a <strong>Charisma check</strong> to understand a few key words of a language you don’t formally know (and/or perhaps speak a couple of stilted words; <em>GM’s call</em>). Additionally, you roll against <strong>Cha</strong> (not <strong>Int</strong>) when recalling lore about history, culture, legends, and etiquette.</p>
