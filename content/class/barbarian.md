<p class="rules-lead">A savage warrior with primal instincts</p>

<div class="rules-prose"><p>You are a <strong>barbarian</strong>, a savage warrior from the tundra, mountains, deep forests, or sandy wastes. <em>You might be a Viking raider, a Conan like behemoth, or an Amazonian warrior woman.</em></p><p>Unlike soft city dwellers, your long exposure to harsh elements has equipped you with primal ferocity, acute instincts, and formidable endurance. You are well versed in the use of weapons, but prefer armours that do not hinder your natural athleticism. Most barbarians are superstitious, illiterate, and have an abiding distrust of magic.</p></div></div>

<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Ferocious Rage</h3>

<p>As a barbarian you may harness a primal and ferocious rage, transforming you into a fearsome and bloodthirsty combatant.</p>
<p>You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. Unless noted otherwise, you may expend a use of this ability as part of your normal action to rage. When you do so, choose one effect from the list below to apply. If an effect requires a <strong>Con check</strong>, you may use a <strong>Reroll</strong>.</p>
<p>Your rage may be loud (bellowing, etc) or quiet (burning glare, etc) which you decide each time you invoke it. You may regain expended uses by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Gain a +2 bonus on melee attack and <strong>damage rolls</strong>, and <strong>Luck rolls</strong>. Also reroll any <strong>fumbled attacks</strong>. These effects last a number of rounds equal to your <strong>Con modifier</strong>.</li>
<li>If you fail a <strong>Str check</strong> attempting a feat of physical power <em>(excluding attacks)</em>, you may reroll it <em>(does not cost a Reroll)</em>.</li>
<li><strong>Once per combat</strong>, when you suffer damage, you may trigger your rage to reduce that damage by half.</li>
<li><strong>Once per target</strong>, after causing damage with a melee weapon, you may attempt a <strong>Con check</strong> to also inflict a relevant <strong>Trauma roll</strong>.</li>
<li>When you are subject to a mental effect (magical or otherwise) such as fear, charm, confusion, or compulsion, you may trigger your rage to attempt a <strong>Con check</strong> to negate it. Madness cannot be negated using this ability.</li>
</ul>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Wild Born</h3>

<p>At <strong>1st level</strong>, your athleticism and survival skills grant you adv on <strong>Wilderness Lore</strong> checks, light armour grants <strong>+3 AC</strong>* instead of <strong>+1 AC</strong>, and you ignore skill penalties for medium armour (p.61).</p>

</section>

</section>
