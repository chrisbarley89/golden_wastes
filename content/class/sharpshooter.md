<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Skirmish Archery</h3>

<p>Where most try to stay as far as possible from their foes with a ranged weapon, you don&#x27;t mind being right in the thick of things. Whether from afar or right in the melee, you have learned to rain death on your enemies with a variety of techniques and specialized ammo.</p>
<p>At <strong>1st level</strong> you know a number of techniques equal to your <strong>Perception modifier</strong>; roll <code>1d12</code> on the list to determine which. With each new level you learn one additional technique of your choice.</p>
<p>You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. Unless noted otherwise, you may expend a use of this ability as part of your normal action. You may regain expended uses by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>. Despite the names of some of these abilities having arrow in them, they can be used with any ranged weapon, even thrown ones.</p>

<div class="rules-talents">
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-1">Blunt Arrow</h4>
After hitting with a ranged attack the target must make a <strong>Luck (Con) save</strong> or be <strong>Dazed</strong>. If you reduce the target to 0 <strong>HP</strong> you may knock them <strong>unconscious</strong> and if you inflict trauma, roll on the <strong>Blunt Trauma table</strong>.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-2">Bomb Arrow</h4>
After hitting with a ranged attack the target must make a <strong>Luck (Str) save</strong> or be knocked <strong>prone</strong> or pushed a short distance. If you inflict trauma, roll on the <strong>Blast Trauma table</strong>.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-3">Point Blank Shooting</h4>
This turn your ranged attacks do not suffer <strong>disadvantage</strong> when made in melee and you gain the rogue&#x27;s <em>Skirmisher</em> ability against targets of your ranged attacks.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-4">Rapid Reload</h4>
You reload a crossbow in the same action you fire it.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">05</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-5">Ricochet Shot</h4>
When making a ranged attack you may ignore the target having total cover. Or after hitting with a ranged attack, you may repeat the attack with <strong>disadvantage</strong> against another target within <strong>Melee range</strong> of the original. At the <em>GM&#x27;s discretion</em> some targets behind total cover may require a successful <strong>Perception (Detection) check</strong> before they can be attacked.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">06</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-6">Serrated Arrow</h4>
After hitting with a ranged attack the target must make a <strong>Luck (Con) save</strong> or start bleeding, taking <code>1d4</code> damage at the start of each of its turns unless it is staunched in some way. If you inflict trauma, roll on the <strong>Blade Trauma table</strong>.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">07</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-7">Shot at a Rescue</h4>
You may make a <strong>Rescue attempt</strong> as long as it can be achieved with a ranged weapon such as pinning an ally&#x27;s cloak to the wall right before they fall in a pit. You test <strong>Perception</strong> (<strong>Detection</strong>) instead of <strong>Initiative</strong> and this ability may only be used once per adventure.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">08</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-8">Shot in the Dark</h4>
This turn any ranged attacks you make are <strong>Perception (Detection) checks</strong> instead, ignoring all vision and range based penalties, with success dealing normal damage.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">09</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-9">Shot on the Run</h4>
This turn, making ranged attacks does not end your Move.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">10</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-10">Snapshot</h4>
During a surprise round if you have a ranged weapon handy you may make a <strong>Perception (Detection) check</strong> to gain a <strong>free attack</strong> with it before anything else is resolved.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">11</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-11">Take the High Ground</h4>
This turn you may climb at your normal <strong>Movement speed</strong> as long as you have one hand free. Given sufficient handholds and places to jump and kick off, no checks are needed during this climb.
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">12</span>
<h4 id="level-1-gain-1-option-12">Trick Shot</h4>
<strong>Once per target</strong> per combat, instead of rolling damage for a ranged attack you may choose effect 1 from the <strong>Blade Trauma table</strong> (str 13) or effect 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 or 8 from the <strong>Missile Trauma table</strong>.
</section>
</div>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Deadly Aim</h3>

<p>When you make a ranged attack, targets lose any <strong>AC</strong> bonus due to cover. In addition, if you miss your target you never reroll the attack against an ally in the same melee. Finally, <strong>Nat 19</strong> effects also happen on a nat 18 with ranged weapons.</p>

</section>

</section>

<h2 id="level-2">Level 2</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-2-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-2-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Battlefield Scavenger</h3>

<p>At <strong>2nd level</strong> you have become adept at recovering ammunition after a battle. Your <strong>Ammunition Rolls</strong> are always a <code>1-in-6</code> chance. After a fight where you would run out of ammo you may make a <strong>Perception (Detection) check</strong> and on a <strong>great success</strong> you find enough ammo to go back to making <strong>Ammunition Rolls</strong>.</p>
<p>Additionally the <strong>GM</strong> may call for you to make a <strong>Perception (Detection) check</strong> after a battle and on a success you find an item that is particularly useful to your current goals or situation.</p>

</section>
