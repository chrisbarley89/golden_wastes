<p class="rules-lead">Martial artist, unarmed and dangerous</p>

<div class="rules-prose"><p>You are a <strong>monk</strong>, a pilgrim adventurer and martial artist, travelling the world in search of enlightenment. <em>You might be a peacekeeping vagabond, a reclusive mystic, or a vigilante drifter.</em> Raised by your order, you lack family attachment and eschew material things, focusing instead on the perfect alignment of mind, body, and spirit.</p>
<p>Unarmed and unarmoured, only a fool mistakes you for easy prey. A lifetime of training has forged you into a fearsome weapon; strong as the mountain and supple as the wind, you perform deeds others hold impossible. Your discipline and spirit burn brighter than the sun.</p></div></div>

<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

</div>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Martial Arts</h3>

<p>You are a master of unarmed combat, causing <code>1d6</code> damage (<strong>Nat 19</strong> Blunt Trauma <code>1d12</code>) with your hands, feet, and other body parts. You may apply your <strong>Dex mod</strong> to your melee attack and <strong>damage rolls</strong> (armed or unarmed) rather than <strong>Strength</strong>. You make an <strong>Extra attack</strong> each turn with a punch, kick, headbutt, etc, as if <strong>Two Weapon Fighting</strong>, but this <strong>Extra attack</strong> does not suffer <strong>disadvantage</strong> (p.80).</p>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Monk Techniques</h3>

<p>You employ secret and highly effective fighting techniques, passed down from master to pupil over the centuries. At <strong>1st level</strong>, you know a number of techniques equal to your <strong>Will modifier</strong>; roll <code>1d12</code> on the below list to determine which. With each new level, learn one additional technique of your choosing.</p>
<p>You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. Unless noted otherwise, you may expend a use of this ability as part of your normal action, choosing one technique to apply. If a technique requires a <strong>Will check</strong>, you may use a <strong>Reroll</strong>. You may regain expended uses by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>.</p>

<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
<h4 id="ability-formless-water-4e5048d7297d">Formless Water</h4>
<p>Until the start of your next turn, gain a bonus equal to your <strong>Will mod</strong> to <strong>AC</strong>, <strong>Luck (Dex) saves</strong>, and defensive <strong>Dex checks</strong>. You may trigger this ability after being hit or otherwise targeted by an opponent (potentially turning a hit into a miss, etc).</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
<h4 id="ability-spirit-strike-58f369ed4293">Spirit Strike</h4>
<p>For <code>2d6</code> rounds, your melee attacks count as magical, cause <strong>critical hits</strong> on <strong>natural 19-20</strong> <strong>attack rolls</strong>, and suppress target regeneration.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
<h4 id="ability-moon-shields-sun-bd0ade6dff18">Moon Shields Sun</h4>
<p>You may trigger this ability when hit by a non magical ranged attack (thrown dagger, arrow, etc). On a successful <strong>Will check</strong>, you deflect the attack, turning it into a miss. <strong>Critical hits</strong> cannot be deflected. This ability may not be used more than once per round.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
<h4 id="ability-heaven-s-leap-ff10ba9ee4b9">Heaven&#x27;s Leap</h4>
<p>You jump up to <strong>Close range</strong> in any direction. <em>GM discretion</em> applies, and a staff, creature, or other terrain might be required to launch from. You gain the Rogue <em>Skirmisher</em> ability until the end of your turn.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">05</span>
<h4 id="ability-strength-of-one-e9acd7f30794">Strength of One</h4>
<p>Gain <strong>Str 19</strong> for one action.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">06</span>
<h4 id="ability-mind-over-matter-11e6544f8a08">Mind Over Matter</h4>
<p>You delay the effect of a persistent physical injury (eg a Trauma or Injuries &amp; Setbacks result) until the next <strong>Long Rest</strong>. Only one injury may be delayed at a time.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">07</span>
<h4 id="ability-purity-of-self-131846d53c14">Purity of Self</h4>
<p>When subject to a disease or poison (including magical), you may trigger this ability to make a <strong>Will check</strong> to negate it.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">08</span>
<h4 id="ability-moment-of-clarity-94140a75caa5">Moment of Clarity</h4>
<p>Until the end of your next turn, you ignore penalties due to poor visibility, and may make a <strong>Will check</strong> to pinpoint invisible or hidden foes within <strong>Far range</strong>.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">09</span>
<h4 id="ability-perfection-of-will-a0b54b34e634">Perfection of Will</h4>
<p>When required to make a <strong>Luck (Will) save</strong>, you may trigger this ability to make a <strong>Will check</strong> instead.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">10</span>
<h4 id="ability-fork-the-river-6bf3c7b657e0">Fork the River</h4>
<p>When damaged by a melee attack, you may trigger this ability to make a <strong>Will check</strong> to transfer the damage to another enemy instead (within <strong>Melee range</strong> of yourself or the original attacker). This ability may not be used more than once per round.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">11</span>
<h4 id="ability-dream-sight-2d310e970f0d">Dream Sight</h4>
<p>You may attempt a <strong>Will check</strong> to sense magic, treat as Pierce the Veil, but this ability is not magical.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">12</span>
<h4 id="ability-iron-fist-2908fc2f54b0">Iron Fist</h4>
<p>On a failed <strong>Luck (Will) save</strong>, one living target within <strong>Melee range</strong> is <strong>Stunned</strong>. Large creatures may gain a bonus on the save, and very large creatures may be immune (<em>GM’s call</em>).</p>
</section>

<p>At <strong>5th level</strong>, you unlock glimmers of spiritual enlightenment, invigorating your physical and mental being. Gain the following benefits:</p>
<ul>
<li>You unarmed attacks cause <code>1d10</code> damage instead of <code>1d6</code>.</li>
<li><em>Open Hand Versatility</em> applies on a <strong>Natural 19-20</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Once per adventure</strong>, after a period of meditation and contemplation, you may spend a <strong>Reroll</strong> to gain a useful insight. Bearing in mind your party goals, the <strong>GM</strong> will remind you of an important piece of information, spotlight an overlooked clue or connection, or provide some other kind of inspiration.</li>
<li><strong>Once per adventure</strong>, during your turn you may spend a <strong>Reroll</strong> to make a <strong>Will check</strong>. If successful, one magical effect you are subject to ends. If others are subject to the same effect, it does not end for them.</li>
</ul>
