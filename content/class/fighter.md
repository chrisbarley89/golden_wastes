<p class="rules-lead">Warrior trained in weapons and fighting styles</p>

<div class="rules-prose"><p>You are a <strong>fighter</strong>, a veteran warrior expertly trained in weapons and armour. <em>Perhaps you are a mail clad knight, an agile spearman, or a sharp eyed bowman.</em> Fighters are versatile masters of all weapons, switching between armaments and combat styles to best meet the foe at hand.</p>
<p>The fighter is the most heavily armed and armoured adventurer in the game. You are comfortable in the thick of battle, relying on brawn, steel, and superior skill to win the day.</p></div></div>

<h2 id="level-1">Level 1</h2>

<a href='#class-progression'>↑ Progression</a>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-1">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-1"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Adaptable</h3>

<p>Your combat expertise allows you to switch weapons and fighting styles/stances at a moment’s notice. At <strong>1st level</strong>, you know a number of styles equal to your <strong>Strength modifier</strong>; roll <code>1d12</code> on the below list to determine which. With each new level, learn one additional style of your choosing.</p>
<p>You begin an adventure with one use of this ability per level. Unless noted otherwise, you may expend a use of this ability as part of your normal action, choosing one stance to apply. You may regain expended uses by taking <strong>Short or Long Rests</strong>.</p>
<p>At <strong>1st level</strong>, choose one of your styles to be your default style. You gain the benefits of your default style at all times, except when you switch to another using <em>Adaptable</em>. You may change your default style when you level up, if you wish.</p>
<p>When you expend a use of <em>Adaptable</em>, choose one of your styles and apply its benefits until the end of that combat. Stance benefits do not stack with each other, only the current stance applies. If swapping armaments, you stow any readied gear as part of your action. You may revert to your default style at any time during your turn (no action, and this does not expend a use of <em>Adaptable</em>).</p>

<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
<h4 id="ability-charger-d996c2501c7f">Charger</h4>
<p>When you take the charge action, enemies do not gain the usual <strong>+2 attack bonus</strong> against you, and if you hit, the target must make a <strong>Luck (Str) save</strong> or be knocked <strong>prone</strong> and/or pushed a short distance.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
<h4 id="ability-dual-weapons-a668377b3686">Dual Weapons</h4>
<p>When <strong>Two Weapon Fighting</strong>, every time you miss with your <strong>Extra attack</strong>, you gain +1 to hit (cumulative) on subsequent <strong>Extra attacks</strong> until it hits.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
<h4 id="ability-long-reach-cd1c491dc615">Long Reach</h4>
<p>When using a <strong>Second Rank weapon</strong>, you may make a free melee attack when you first engage a foe. <strong>Once per battle</strong> only (ie not per foe).</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
<h4 id="ability-opportunist-01122f08aaf6">Opportunist</h4>
<p>When you reduce a foe to zero hit points with a melee or thrown attack, immediately make another melee or thrown attack. You may activate this style when you reduce a target to zero hit points (no action, but must be during your turn). You may not use this ability more than once per round.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">05</span>
<h4 id="ability-protector-70b4409abd52">Protector</h4>
<p>You automatically succeed on <strong>Initiative checks</strong> to trigger <strong>Rescue Exploits</strong>, and shields grant <strong>+2 AC</strong> instead of +1.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">06</span>
<h4 id="ability-ranged-35c795f86a59">Ranged</h4>
<p>When you make a ranged or thrown attack, targets lose any <strong>AC</strong> bonus due to cover. Additionally if you miss your target you never reroll the attack against an ally in the same melee.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">07</span>
<h4 id="ability-single-weapon-ffbd6daa8d25">Single Weapon</h4>
<p>When armed with a one handed weapon and your other hand empty <em>(exception: holding a light)</em>, gain +2 <strong>Init</strong>, <strong>defensive Luck (Dex) rolls</strong>, &amp; <strong>Party Retreats</strong>.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">08</span>
<h4 id="ability-two-hander-fbcee0b03d56">Two Hander</h4>
<p>When using a melee weapon two handed, gain adv when rolling damage.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">09</span>
<h4 id="ability-unarmed-7b94491f9453">Unarmed</h4>
<p>Your unarmed attacks cause <code>1d6</code> damage instead of <code>1d2</code>. On a <strong>Nat 19</strong>, you may grab or disarm your foe.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">10</span>
<h4 id="ability-brutal-c91229b6b5b2">Brutal</h4>
<p>When you inflict a <strong>Nat 19</strong> effect with a melee weapon, add +2 to <strong>Trauma rolls</strong> (does not stack with similar modifiers).</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">11</span>
<h4 id="ability-rearguard-515b45f961d2">Rearguard</h4>
<p>You may activate this style at the start of a round, prior to your turn. You adopt defensive manoeuvres, directing a tactical retreat as you guide your allies to safety. All party members gain <strong>advantage</strong> on all <strong>Party Retreat</strong> related tests.</p>
</section>
<section class="rules-talent">
<span class="rules-talent__number" aria-hidden="true">12</span>
<h4 id="ability-expert-guard-6f7c89c56c8c">Expert Guard</h4>
<p>Your fumbled weapon attacks become ordinary misses instead. You may activate this style when you roll a <strong>fumble</strong>.</p>
</section>
</div>

</section>

<section class="rules-ability" aria-labelledby="level-1-gain-2">

<h3 id="level-1-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Deadly Strikes</h3>

<p>Your battle training helps you land crippling blows. When using a weapon, your <strong>crit range</strong> expands to 19-20, and <strong>Nat 19</strong> effects also occur on a crit.</p>

<h3 id="level-2-gain-2"><svg class="rules-gain__icon" viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"><path d="M8 3v10M3 8h10" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>Supplies</h3>

<p>By <strong>2nd level</strong>, you naturally anticipate and prepare for dangers and obstacles that lie ahead. At any time during an adventure, you may automatically add <code>1d4</code> mundane items to your equipment list (the items must have a common theme). For example, you might gain additional coils of rope, a grappling hook and climbing boots, a cache of fire pots, and so on. Particularly expensive or bulky items may not be permitted at the <em>GM’s discretion</em>. After using this ability, you may not use it again until you resupply at a village, town or city (the <strong>GM</strong> might impose additional limits).</p>
