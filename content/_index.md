Welcome to the [Golden Wastes](/location/the-wastes/), a post-apocalyptic fantasy setting created for [TTRPG play](/campaign/how-to-play/) and world-building.

The [project](/campaign/the-project/) explores a brutally harsh region between one and three centuries after the global apocalypse known as the [Second Great Pulse](/lore/great-pulse/).

Scorching salt flats, towering teeth-like formations and all kinds of riches stretch as far as the eye can see, while far below lies a colossal network of caverns known simply as [The Beneath](/location/the-beneath/).
